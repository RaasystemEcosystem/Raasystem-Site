FAQ

RaasGenAIOS — AI Operating System

What is RaasGenAIOS? +

RaasGenAIOS is a sovereign, AI-native operating system that governs intelligence, decision-making, and execution across the Raasystem ecosystem. It functions as the central nervous system coordinating applications, blockchain infrastructure, cloud compute, GPU resources, and real-world assets through deterministic and auditable AI execution.

Is RaasGenAIOS an application or a platform? +

Neither. RaasGenAIOS is an AI Operating System. It governs intent routing, simulation, policy enforcement, and execution across entire digital ecosystems. Raasystem itself is one of many intelligent applications running on top of RaasGenAIOS.

Does RaasGenAIOS replace Android, iOS, Windows, or macOS? +

No. RaasGenAIOS sits above existing operating systems as an AI Operating Layer. Traditional OSes manage devices and user interfaces, while RaasGenAIOS manages intelligence, workflows, governance, and ecosystem-wide coordination across them.

Can RaasGenAIOS run independently without another OS? +

Yes — in controlled environments like AI nodes, edge appliances, embedded systems, and sovereign infrastructure. On consumer devices, it operates above existing OSes to maximize adoption, interoperability, and scalability.

Why is RaasGenAIOS considered a world-first? +

It is the first OS designed with AI as the governing authority rather than a feature. It introduces intent-driven execution, deterministic AI workflows, governance-before-execution, and strict separation between intelligence and on-chain execution — enabling institutional-grade trust across digital and real-world systems.

Core Ecosystem Components

What is RaasMatrix? +

RaasMatrix is the programmable trust and governance layer of Raasystem. It generates, registers, governs, and deploys smart contracts proposed by RaasGenAIOS, ensuring deterministic execution, upgradeability, and institutional-grade auditability.

What is RaasBridge? +

RaasBridge is a cross-chain and cross-asset orchestration layer that enables interoperability across blockchains, asset classes, and infrastructures under AI-driven coordination.

What is RaasCampus? +

RaasCampus is the real-world asset (RWA) and physical infrastructure arm of Raasystem, covering initiatives such as agriculture, data centers, healthcare, and regional digital infrastructure across Africa.

What is Raastudio? +

Raastudio is the innovation and creation environment within Raasystem where developers, creators, and partners build, test, and deploy applications, with a strong focus on African creative economies.

Platform & Ecosystem

What is Rabex Hallmark? +

Rabex Hallmark is an automated market maker (AMM) platform that enables autonomous and AI-guided trading, allowing smart, efficient, and fully automated exchange of assets.

Why does Raasystem have many platforms? +

Raasystem’s multi-platform design ensures sovereignty, modularity, and economies of scale, enabling specialized platforms to operate independently while remaining fully integrated into the ecosystem.

Why Two Tokens: Raas and RBT? +

Raasystem uses a two-token economy: RBT serves as the operational currency for transactions, while Raas acts as a stable settlement currency, ensuring stability and flexibility throughout the ecosystem.

Is RaasExplorer different from other explorers? +

Yes — RaasExplorer provides advanced analytics, real-time insights, and comprehensive transparency, going beyond traditional blockchain explorers.

What makes Raaspay different from traditional payment gateways? +

Raaspay is an AI-driven, hybrid blockchain payment infrastructure that delivers high-speed settlements, cross-chain interoperability, and real-world asset integration — enabling seamless, secure, and scalable transactions across borders.

Why does Raasystem integrate with AWS, Nvidia, XDC, XRP, USDT, and ICE? +

These integrations extend capability, liquidity, and real-world utility. AI-driven coordination ensures every external interaction strengthens the ecosystem while preserving autonomy, security, and governance integrity.

How does Raasystem compare to Robinhood, Binance, Coinbase, Nasdaq, and NYSE? +

Raasystem combines stocks, commodities, crypto, and cross-chain access in one AI-orchestrated ecosystem. Users benefit from integrated wallets, trading, payments, analytics, and governance tools — giving more flexibility and autonomy than peers individually.

Access, Security & Onboarding

What is RaasWallet? +

RaasWallet is the primary gateway into Raasystem. It provides unified access to trading, payments, governance, analytics, and ecosystem services through a single secure dashboard.

How does user onboarding work? +

Users onboard through RaasWallet, which grants access to RaasBridge, trading platforms, payment services, analytics, and governance tools — all orchestrated by RaasGenAIOS.

How does Raasystem ensure security? +

Security is enforced at the AI Operating System level. RaasGenAIOS provides continuous monitoring, threat detection, policy enforcement, and deterministic execution across all ecosystem layers.

What makes Raasystem difficult to replicate? +

Raasystem combines sovereign AI governance, programmable trust, cross-chain infrastructure, and real-world asset integration into a single operating fabric — a level of systemic integration that is extremely difficult to reproduce.
